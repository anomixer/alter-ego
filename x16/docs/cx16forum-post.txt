Alter Ego for the Commander X16 - 26-level puzzle-platformer, SD-card build

[b]Alter Ego[/b] is a puzzle-platformer from 2011 - originally a ZX Spectrum game by
Denis Grachev, later converted to the NES by Shiru. This is a Commander X16 port of the
NES version: 26 levels, title music, SFX, the pause menu, the credits and the ending, all
ported over intact.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anomixer/alter-ego/master/x16/docs/forum/title.png[/img]

[b]The one rule[/b]

You cannot jump. Your only move is the [b]exchange[/b]: you trade places with your mirror
image, your alter ego. Press B and you are standing at the mirrored spot while the copy
takes your place - mirror left-to-right in most levels, top-to-bottom in a few
(LV05/08/15/19/24), and that difference is the whole game. Read where the mirror lands,
swap at the right moment, and you cross the gap, reach the ledge or drop past what
patrols the level. Level 1 spells out HELLO WORLD for a reason, and the later ones
(they have names like PROMENADE) start to bite.

Unzip the archive into the [b]root[/b] of the card - there is no folder inside it - and the
game is installed. At the BASIC prompt, with the card as drive 8:

[code]
LOAD "LOADER.PRG",8,1
RUN
[/code]

Rename LOADER.PRG to AUTOBOOT.X16 and it starts by itself at power-on.

[b]Controls[/b]

[list]
[*]arrow keys - move and climb (hold the arrow and the hero keeps walking)
[*]X or J - A / action (confirm, use)
[*]Z or K - B / exchange (the swap with your alter ego)
[*]Enter or Space - START (title menu: start game, start level, resume from pause)
[*]1 or Left Shift - SELECT (there is a COW LEVEL behind it)
[*]M / N - music mute / SFX mute (the title screen shows MUSIC OFF / SOUND OFF at once)
[*]T - SFX/BGM test screen (T or ESC leaves)
[/list]

SNES pads in ports 1-4 work as an alternative to the keyboard. Title menu: up/down pick
the line, left/right change the option or the level number. And yes, there is a level-skip
cheat: inside a level, Z, Left, X, Z, Left, X, then 1.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anomixer/alter-ego/master/x16/docs/forum/soundtest.png[/img]

[b]What you need[/b]

Any stock Commander X16 (VERA plus the 512 KB of banked RAM the game stages its tile and
level data in). No DOS, no disk writes - the game only reads its files from drive 8
through the KERNAL. It is the NES screen at 320x240, drawn with 8x8 VERA tiles and
hardware sprites and scaled 2x to 640x480; the music and SFX are FamiTone data played on
the VERA PSG (16 channels) by a small interpreter written for this port, and the 60 Hz
logic loop comes off the VERA VSYNC interrupt.

[b]Honest status[/b]

Verified on x16emu r49 (with HostFS) and on the r49 browser build: boot, title menu, level
1 and several later levels, the exchange, the pause menu, the special keys, the credits
and the ending. [b]Not yet verified on real hardware[/b] - I do not own an X16 - so reports
from real machines are very welcome, in particular from a real SD card with cmdr-dos and
how the PSG music sounds on a real unit.
